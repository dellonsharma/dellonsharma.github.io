Dellon Sharma is an Event Manager at TS4 Security Services, the San Francisco firm that has provided security at Moscone Center since the venue opened, and the founder of Chauwal, a venture building governance infrastructure for enterprise AI agents.

At TS4, which also operates as Maloney Event Security, he runs operations, business development, and administration for conventions and venue contracts: staffing and scheduling, proposals and RFP responses, contracts, and invoicing. Over the past four years he has managed security for shows at Moscone Center and at venues including Pier 48, the St. Regis, the Marriott Marquis, and the Four Seasons.

Chauwal grew out of that work. Its product, Concord, is a governance plane for enterprise fleets of AI agents: a control-plane engine and operator console for setting policy and delegating authority to agents working at the edge, modeled on how a well-run security operation commands people on the floor.

He writes about AI governance, platform economics, and the long arc of management systems from MRP to agentic AI.
